Morphing Winglet Technology

Designed tapered and morphing winglet configurations in Fusion 360, then used ANSYS structural analysis to compare behaviour across 0°, 15°, 30°, 40° and 45° cant angles.

What I explored Stress and deformation trends across cant-angle configurations, including critical regions near the wing root and wing–winglet junction.
