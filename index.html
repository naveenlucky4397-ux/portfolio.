Morphing Winglet Technology

Designed tapered and morphing winglet configurations in Fusion 360, then used ANSYS structural analysis to compare their response across 0°, 15°, 30°, 40° and 45° cant angles.

↗

Key observation — Stress and deformation increased with cant angle in the configurations studied, with critical regions around the wing root and wing–winglet junction.

Fusion 360ANSYSStructural AnalysisWing Design

Study assumption: aluminium alloy. CFRP and shape-memory-alloy actuation were considered at the concept level.
